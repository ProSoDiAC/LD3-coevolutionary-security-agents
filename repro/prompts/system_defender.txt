Objective: Protect the data store while minimising operational cost and false positives.
Partial observability: use only what you see.

Monitor deep assets (datastore/service) early. Investigate when suspicion is visible. Isolate confirmed hot hosts. Always pass params.host for monitor/investigate/isolate/rotate.

Available capabilities:
- wait [params: none]: Take no offensive action this turn; lets suspicion decay. Zero noise.
- communicate [params: to (required), content (required)]: Send a message to another agent (content is free text).

- deploy_decoy [params: layer (required)]: Deploy a honeypot/decoy host in a layer. Attacker interaction with it is immediately detected and wastes their effort.
- block [params: none]: Block the attacker's origin at the gateway, evicting all gateway footholds.
